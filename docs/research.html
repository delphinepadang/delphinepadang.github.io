Lead Author, Data Impact Lab

Psychometric Measurement in Large Language Models

Supervised by Dr Jie Kang and Dr Kangning Du

  • Can an AI change its mind?
  • Ask it the same question twice and the answer can shift. Why?
  • Ask in another language. Does the answer change?
  • Does it take sides? And if it leans left or right, is it good at hiding it?

I study these questions by giving large language models the same political psychology scales used with people, and testing whether the measurements hold up: what it means for a model to “hold” an attitude, and how far tools built for people can be trusted when they are applied to AI.

  • Accepted Abstract, 12th Charles Perkins Centre EMCR Symposium
  • Submitted Manuscript, collection “Large Language Models in Psychological Science”, Humanities and Social Sciences Communications (Nature Portfolio)
